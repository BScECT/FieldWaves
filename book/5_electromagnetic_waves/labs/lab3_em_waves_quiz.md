### Lab 3: Formative assessment

:::{admonition} Constants
:class: note

Use $c=3.00\times10^8\ \mathrm{m/s}$, $\eta_0=\sqrt{\mu_0/\epsilon_0}\simeq377\ \Omega$, $\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{H/m}$ and $\epsilon_0=8.854\times10^{-12}\ \mathrm{F/m}$.
:::

A ground-penetrating radar is towed on a small boat across a freshwater lake to map the depth of the lake bed. Its antenna, just above the water, sends a $100\ \mathrm{MHz}$ wave straight down, along $+z$. The lake water is non-magnetic, with $\epsilon'=81\,\epsilon_0$ and conductivity $\sigma=0.01\ \mathrm{S/m}$. Its dielectric loss is negligible.

(a) Compare $\sigma$ with $\omega\epsilon'$. Is the lake water a good conductor or a low-loss dielectric at this frequency? (2 points)

(b) For the reflection at the water surface, treat the water as lossless, with $\eta_w=\eta_0/\sqrt{\epsilon_r}$. Find the reflection coefficient $r$ and the reflected and transmitted power fractions $\mathcal R$ and $\mathcal T$ at normal incidence. (2 points)

(c) In a low-loss dielectric, $\alpha\simeq\dfrac{\sigma}{2}\sqrt{\dfrac{\mu_0}{\epsilon'}}$. Find the phase velocity $v_p$, the wavelength $\lambda$, the attenuation constant $\alpha$ and the field attenuation depth $\delta_E$ in the water. (2 points)

(d) The echo from the lake bed arrives $300\ \mathrm{ns}$ after the echo from the water surface. How deep is the lake? By what factor does absorption in the water reduce the power of the lake-bed echo? (2 points)

(e) Suppose the lake bed reflected all the power reaching it. Ignoring spreading, what fraction of the incident power density returns to the antenna? Use $\mathcal T$ from (b) for both crossings of the water surface. Would the survey work on a saline lake with $\sigma=4\ \mathrm{S/m}$, where the skin depth is about $2.5\ \mathrm{cm}$? (2 points)
