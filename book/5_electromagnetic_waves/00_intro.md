# Electromagnetic waves

This chapter develops electromagnetic waves from Maxwell’s equations and applies them to radar measurements. We begin with the experiments that connected electricity and magnetism, then derive plane waves and relate their electric and magnetic fields through the wave impedance.

Next, we introduce harmonic waves and phasors, connect the field equations to voltage and current on transmission lines, and use radar delay and phase to measure distance and motion. We then extend the picture to waves spreading in three dimensions, energy flow and polarization, showing how propagation and field orientation affect what an antenna measures. The lab exercises put these ideas into practice.
