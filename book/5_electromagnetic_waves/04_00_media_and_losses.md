# Propagation through media

Our radar wave has reached the ground. What happens next depends on what the ground is made of. The same signal can travel quite happily through one material, arrive late through another, or fade away before it reaches anything interesting.

We connect that behaviour to the material's electric and magnetic response. First we change the wave's speed without losing energy. Then we let the dielectric response dissipate energy, add conduction as another loss mechanism, and recover the diffusion equation and skin depth. Radar penetration gives the calculations a purpose throughout.

Keep using complex fields: the common time factor is $e^{j\omega t}$, and forward propagation has $e^{-jkz}$. At first we take the field **just inside** a material as given. The next section will ask how much gets in and how much reflects.
