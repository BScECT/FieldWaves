Model parameters, scope and equations

All presets are illustrative, not measured material fits. The soil examples have constant ε′ and constant dielectric loss ratio over this displayed band. Dry: ε′/ε₀=4, ε″d/ε′=0.005, σ=0.0001 S/m. Wetter: ε′/ε₀=16, ε″d/ε′=0.03, σ=0.02 S/m.

Both water-like examples use εd/ε₀ = 5 + 75/(1+jωτ), τ=9 ps; conductivity is 0.005 S/m for the freshwater-like example and 4 S/m for the saline example. This choice isolates added ionic conduction while holding the molecular response fixed. It does not reproduce the full temperature/salinity dependence of real water. For quantitative models see Meissner and Wentz (2004).

εeff = εd − jσ/ω; k² = ω²μ₀εeff; δE = 1/α, with k=β−jα. The dashed depth is √[2/(ωμ₀σ)] and requires σ much greater than ω|εd|. The criterion uses the complete dielectric current, including dielectric loss. One-way power depth is δE/2. Down-and-back absorption factors at depth d are e⁻²ᵅᵈ for field and e⁻⁴ᵅᵈ for power. No surface transmission, reflection strength, scattering, spreading, instrument noise or frequency-dependent σ is modelled.
