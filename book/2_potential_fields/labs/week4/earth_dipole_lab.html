Drawn as in the lecture slides: m̂ = ẑ points up the page and θ is measured from m̂. For the present Earth, m̂ points to the southern end of the dipole axis, so the southern end (θ = 0) is at the top and the northern end (θ = 180°) at the bottom. Arrows at the probe show the bracket 3(m̂·r̂)r̂ − m̂ (blue: 3(m̂·r̂)r̂, red: −m̂, black: sum); their length does not depend on r. The field itself is B = B*(a/r)3 times this bracket, with B* = 29.7 µT and a = 6371 km.
