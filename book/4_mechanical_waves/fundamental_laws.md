# Seismic wave fields from fundamental physical laws

Above, we discussed the kinematic properties of wave motion, using rays and traveltimes, without worrying ourselves about the more fundamental laws that were behind it. Here we start from the physical fundamentals of mechanics, i.e., Newton's second law and deformation laws. Using these laws, we can derive the basic equations for wave motion, which then result in the so-called wave equation. The solution of the wave equation shows the features as discussed previously: it turns out that rays are obtained from the high-frequency approximation to that wave equation. The attenuation of the wave due to spreading is captured by the wave equation. And reflection and transmission amplitudes are also captured by applying the resulting equations describing the wave motion. So, here we will go into this, giving the fundamental backbone to the seismic wavefield recordings.
