# Seismic wavefields

In this chapter, we discuss seismic wave fields. We start from a kinematic point of view, including basic notions of wave fronts, rays, travel times, Huygens' principle and Snell's law at boundaries. Commonly, seismic wavefield measurements are taking place at the surface and then typically direct waves, reflected waves and critically-refracted/head waves are observed, and therefore considered here. Then, some typical wavefield recordings as observed on land and at sea are interpreted, in terms of these arrivals. From these events velocities and depths can be estimated. And from those, a first model for the subsurface is obtained (where the model is here seen as the interpretation of the wavefield data).

Next, the seismic wave fields are discussed from a dynamic point of view. There we use the fundamental laws of Newton and Hooke to derive the basic equations describing seismic wave fields. From these the wave equation can be derived. Next, reflection and transmission coefficients at boundaries are derived. And finally elastic media with longitudinal (P) and shear (S) waves are considered and which equations describe their full dynamic behaviour.
