# Weekly schedule

Each unit runs over one week and consists of three lessons, numbered after the unit. Unit 1.4, for example, is made up of lessons 1.4.1, 1.4.2 and 1.4.3. The first two are lectures and the third is the practical, where the computer labs in this book are used.

[My Timetable](https://mytimetable.tudelft.nl/) carries the authoritative schedule, with rooms and any changes.
